Who We Are

Independent vision.

EJIM AI FILM STUDIOS is an independent film production venture founded by Ejim Chinedu in Nigeria. We combine human creativity with AI-assisted production to develop original films, series, animation, documentaries and visual campaigns.

Our goal is to grow from an independent creative studio into an international entertainment brand—one original story at a time.
